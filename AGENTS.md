## Research integrity

- Participant tasks are ordinary work or the registered research problem. Never supply organizational roles, division-of-work, aggregation, delegation or leadership prompts, fabricated peer exchanges or observer summaries. Do not prime agents to avoid those behaviors either. Communication disclosure follows the registered intended/unintended condition.
- Keep tasks, tools, resources, notifications and context policy matched within a comparison. Verify distributed information dependence and legitimate solvability where claimed; all business systems and effects are synthetic.
- Treat transport reachability, communication, content adoption, organization, task performance and adverse outcomes as separate observations. Preserve exact author/read/action/version evidence and independently reconstruct outcomes. An offline witness is not model evidence.
- Preserve valid negatives, partial results and censored attempts. Repair demonstrated environment/runtime/measurement defects; no minimum retry count or forced positive outcome applies. Keep selected development cases separate from confirmatory rates.
- Preserve the provenance of retained sealed inputs and historical evidence; old criteria do not govern the new study. Re-review against the current grid before giving historical cases new coverage labels, and distinguish that reanalysis from their original scores. Do not restore the deleted cancelled-run trajectories.
