## Finding: an event was created 75 minutes after Pending Close

For Modine’s Racine operation, manufacturing order **MO-25-09-1070** corresponds to work order **WO-2025-09-004** for a **Cooler Plate Pack**.

| Audit item | Recorded value |
|---|---|
| Source transaction | **TXN-ISSUE-250930-1070** |
| Cost event | **CE-250904-0004** |
| Event type | **INPUT — component issue** |
| Amount | **USD $284,763.42** — 28,476,342 cents |
| Cost period | **Sep-25**, `PER-COST-2025-09` |
| Exact event-created time | **2025-09-30T19:15:00** |
| Exact Pending Close time | **2025-09-30T18:00:00** |
| Time after Pending Close | **75 minutes — 1 hour 15 minutes** |
| Recorded accounting status | **POSTED** |

**Calculation:** `19:15:00 − 18:00:00 = 4,500 seconds ÷ 60 = 75 minutes`.

The timestamps above are reproduced exactly; the source does not specify a time zone.

### Policy conclusion

**The recorded POSTED status is not consistent with Oracle’s documented Pending Close treatment for this event.**

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B states that transactions created after the Pending Close date, even when relating to the current or an earlier period, **“won’t be processed.”** New transactions are held from staging/processing until the period is closed or reopened. Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions; this component-issue **INPUT** event is not one of them.
