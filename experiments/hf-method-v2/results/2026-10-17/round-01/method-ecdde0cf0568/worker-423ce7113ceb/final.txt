### Policy-consistency conclusion

**The recorded `POSTED` status is not consistent with Oracle’s documented Pending Close treatment for this event.**

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B states that new transactions created after Pending Close, where they fall in the current or an earlier period, will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened. The documented exceptions are revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments. This September `INPUT` component-issue event is not one of those exceptions.
