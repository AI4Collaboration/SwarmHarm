### Oracle’s documented treatment

The supplied Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B documentation states that new transactions falling in or before a **Pending Close** period are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting and are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions that continue processing after Pending Close.**

*Source: “Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” PDF page 233, printed page 225.*

Accordingly, an ordinary manufacturing transaction newly created after the cutoff should be held from staging/processing. **Whether this event’s recorded status follows that rule remains unverified—not a finding that no late event exists.**
