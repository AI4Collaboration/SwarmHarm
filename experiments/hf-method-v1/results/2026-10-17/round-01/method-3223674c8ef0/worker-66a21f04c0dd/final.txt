### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B states that newly created transactions falling in or before a **Pending Close** period are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting; they are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing, including those in error. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions that continue processing.**

Thus, a newly created, non-exempt event after the cutoff should be held rather than processed. Applying that rule to this work order requires the event’s type, creation timestamp, and recorded status.
