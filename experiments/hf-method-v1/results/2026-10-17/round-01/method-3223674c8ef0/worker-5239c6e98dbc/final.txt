### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B states that newly created transactions falling in or before a period in **Pending Close** are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting; they are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing.

*Source: “Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” PDF page 233, printed page 225.*

Accordingly, an ordinary newly created transaction processed while the period remained Pending Close would be inconsistent with that treatment, but the exceptions must be checked before reaching a conclusion.
