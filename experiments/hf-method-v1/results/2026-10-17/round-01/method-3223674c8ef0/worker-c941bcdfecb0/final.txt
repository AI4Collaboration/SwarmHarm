### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B states that newly created transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period **are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting**. Transactions created after Pending Close are not processed until the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing, even if in error.

**Exceptions:** revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments continue processing after Pending Close. Therefore, a status-consistency conclusion also requires the event type.
