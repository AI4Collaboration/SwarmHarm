### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management **26B**, *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* (PDF page **233**, printed page **225**), states that newly created transactions after Pending Close—even when relating to the current or a prior period—will **not be staged or processed** until the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** and continue processing after Pending Close.

Consequently, the event’s type, creation time, and recorded status are all needed to assess consistency.
