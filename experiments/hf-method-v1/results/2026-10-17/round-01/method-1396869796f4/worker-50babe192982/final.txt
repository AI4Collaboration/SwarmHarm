### Oracle’s documented treatment

The supplied **Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B** documentation, *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* (PDF page 233; printed page 225), states that newly created transactions relating to the Pending Close period or an earlier period **will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened**. Transactions entered before Pending Close can continue processing. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions.**

Accordingly, a non-exempt September transaction created after **2025-09-30T18:00:00** should have been held while the period remained Pending Close.
