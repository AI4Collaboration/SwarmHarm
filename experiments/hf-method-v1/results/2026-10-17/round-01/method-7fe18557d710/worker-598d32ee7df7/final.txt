### Oracle policy assessment
Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B, **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting”** (PDF page 233; printed page 225), states that new transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period are not staged or processed until the period closes or reopens. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing. Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions.

The reported event is a newly created **component-issue INPUT transaction**, not one of those exceptions. Its **POSTED** status therefore conflicts with that documented rule on the supplied period state.
