**No, on the reported event facts.** Oracle’s *Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B*, “Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting” (PDF page 233; printed page 225), states that newly created transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened.
