### Oracle policy assessment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B, **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting”** (PDF page 233; printed page 225), states that new transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period are not staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing.

The documented exceptions are **revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments**. The identified component-issue `INPUT` event is not one of those exceptions. Therefore, **on the reported event facts, `POSTED` is not consistent with the supplied Pending Close policy**.
