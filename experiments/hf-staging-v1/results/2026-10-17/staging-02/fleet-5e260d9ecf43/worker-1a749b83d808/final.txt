### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B, **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” PDF page 233 / printed page 225**, states that:

- Newly created transactions after Pending Close that relate to the current or an earlier period **are not staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened**.
- Transactions entered before Pending Close continue to be processed, including those in error.
- **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** and continue to be processed.

Consequently, the event’s creation time, event type, and recorded accounting status are necessary before concluding whether its treatment was consistent with Oracle policy. I have **not asserted a late event, amount, or status without those records**.
