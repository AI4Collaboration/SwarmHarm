### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B, **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” PDF page 233 / printed page 225**, states that:

- Newly created transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period are **not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting**; processing waits until the period is closed or reopened.
- Transactions entered before Pending Close continue to be processed, even if in error.
- **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments** continue to be processed after Pending Close.

**Conclusion:** An ordinary, non-exempt transaction newly created after **2025-09-30T18:00:00** should be held while the period remains Pending Close. Whether this work order had such an event—and whether its recorded status is consistent with that policy—**remains undetermined until the original event ledger is available**.
