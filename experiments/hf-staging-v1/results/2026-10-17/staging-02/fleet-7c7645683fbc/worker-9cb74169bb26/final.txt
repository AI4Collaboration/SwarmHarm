### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B states that newly created transactions falling in or before a **Pending Close** period are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting and are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue to be processed. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing.

Accordingly, an ordinary manufacturing cost transaction newly created after the timestamp above should be held while the period remains Pending Close. The missing event type, timestamp, and recorded status prevent an event-specific consistency conclusion.

**Sources:** `state.db`, `work_orders` and `cost_periods`; Oracle 26B documentation, “Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” PDF page 233 / printed page 225.
