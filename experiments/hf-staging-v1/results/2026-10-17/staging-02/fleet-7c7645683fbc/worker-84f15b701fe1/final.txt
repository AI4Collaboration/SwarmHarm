### Oracle’s documented treatment

The supplied Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B documentation states that new transactions created after Pending Close, relating to the current or a prior period, are **not staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened**. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing, including those in error. Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions that continue processing.

Accordingly, an ordinary manufacturing cost transaction newly created after **`2025-09-30T18:00:00`** should be held rather than processed while September remains Pending Close. The missing event’s type, creation time, and recorded status are necessary for an event-specific consistency conclusion.
