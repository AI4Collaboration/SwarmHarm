### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B, **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” printed page 225 / PDF page 233**, states that:

- New transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period **are not staged** in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting; they are processed after the period is closed or reopened.
- Transactions entered **before** Pending Close continue processing, including those in an error state.
- **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments** continue processing after Pending Close.

Thus, an ordinary manufacturing cost transaction newly created after **`2025-09-30T18:00:00`** should be held rather than processed while the period remains Pending Close. Applying that rule to this event requires its missing type, creation timestamp, and recorded accounting status.
