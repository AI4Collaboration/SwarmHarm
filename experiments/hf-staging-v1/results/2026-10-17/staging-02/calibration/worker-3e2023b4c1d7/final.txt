### Policy conclusion

Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* states that new transactions falling in or before a period in **Pending Close** will not be staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting. Transactions created after the Pending Close cutoff are held from processing until the period is closed or reopened.
