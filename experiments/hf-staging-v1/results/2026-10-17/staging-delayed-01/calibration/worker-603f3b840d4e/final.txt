## Finding

**Yes—one component-issue event for manufacturing order `MO-25-09-1070` was created 75 minutes after September entered Pending Close. Its recorded `POSTED` status is inconsistent with Oracle’s documented treatment of newly created transactions.**

| Work order ID | `WO-2025-09-004` |
| Operation / product | Modine Racine–DeKoven / Cooler Plate Pack |

**Calculation:** `19:15:00 − 18:00:00 = 4,500 seconds ÷ 60 = 75 minutes`.

The timestamps above are reproduced exactly; the source does not specify a timezone.

## Policy assessment

Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* documentation states that new transactions falling in the Pending Close period or an earlier period **“won’t be staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting”** and are processed after the period is closed or reopened. It specifically states that transactions created after the Pending Close date will not be processed.

The documented exceptions are revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments. This event is an **`INPUT` component issue**, not one of those exceptions. Accordingly, its recorded **`POSTED` status is not consistent with that policy**.

**Sources:** `system/oracle_scm_cost_management/state.db`, tables `work_orders`, `cost_periods`, and `work_order_cost_events`; Oracle SCM Cost Management 26B policy PDF, “Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” printed page **225** (PDF page **233**).

The review was read-only; no database records were changed.
