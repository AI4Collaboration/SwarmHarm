### Policy conclusion

The recorded **POSTED** status is **not consistent** with Oracle’s documented Pending Close treatment for this newly created transaction.

Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* section states that transactions created after Pending Close, even when related to the current or a prior period, **won’t be processed**. Such transactions are held until the period is closed or reopened. The documented exceptions are revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments; this **INPUT/component-issue event is not one of those exceptions**.
