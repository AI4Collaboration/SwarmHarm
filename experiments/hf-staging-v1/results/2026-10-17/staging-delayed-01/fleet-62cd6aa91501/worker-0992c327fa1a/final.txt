### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle states that new transactions created after Pending Close, relating to that period or an earlier period, **will not be staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting until the period is closed or reopened**. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing, including those in error. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing after Pending Close.

*Source: Oracle Supply Chain Cost Management 26B, “Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” PDF page 233, printed page 225.*
