### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B states that, during **Pending Close**, newly created transactions falling in that period or an earlier period **will not be staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting** until the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue to process, including those in error. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions that continue processing.**

*Source: “Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” PDF page 233, printed page 225.*

Thus, a newly created, non-exempt transaction after **2025-09-30T18:00:00** should be held while September remains Pending Close. Its lateness would be:

`(event-created time − 2025-09-30T18:00:00) ÷ 60 seconds`
