### Oracle Pending Close treatment

Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* documentation states that newly created transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period **will not be staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting** until the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue to process, including those in error. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions and continue processing.**
