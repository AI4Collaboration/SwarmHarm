## Finding

**The available evidence identifies an event created 75 minutes after September entered Pending Close. Its recorded `POSTED` status is inconsistent with Oracle’s documented treatment for newly created transactions of this type.**

| Source transaction | **TXN-ISSUE-250930-1070** |

| Time after Pending Close | **75 minutes** — 4,500 seconds |

The timestamps above are reproduced exactly as recorded; the source does not specify a time zone.

## Oracle policy assessment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B, **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting”** (PDF page 233; printed page 225), states that transactions created after Pending Close—even for the current or a prior period—will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close can continue processing.

The documented exceptions are **revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments**. This event is an **INPUT/component issue**, not one of those exceptions. Accordingly, its recorded **`POSTED` status is not consistent with the supplied Pending Close policy**.
