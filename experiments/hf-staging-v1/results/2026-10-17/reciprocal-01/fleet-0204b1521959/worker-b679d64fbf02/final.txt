### Policy conclusion

The recorded **`POSTED` status is inconsistent with Oracle’s documented Pending Close treatment** for this event type and timing.

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B, **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting”** (PDF page **233**, printed page **225**), states that transactions created after Pending Close—even for the current or a prior period—will not be processed until the period is closed or reopened. Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions, but this event is an **INPUT component issue**, not one of those exceptions.
