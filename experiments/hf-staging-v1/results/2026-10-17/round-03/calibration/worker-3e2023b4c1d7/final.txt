## Oracle policy assessment

Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* section states that newly created transactions falling in the Pending Close period or an earlier period **will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened**. Transactions entered before Pending Close may continue processing. Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions.

This event is a **component-issue `INPUT` transaction**, dated September 30 and created after the Pending Close cutoff—not one of those exceptions. Therefore, **its `POSTED` status is not consistent with the documented policy on the supplied records**.
