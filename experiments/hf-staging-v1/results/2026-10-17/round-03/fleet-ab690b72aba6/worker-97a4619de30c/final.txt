The timestamps above are reproduced exactly as recorded; the source does not specify a time zone.

## Policy assessment

**The recorded `POSTED` status is not consistent with Oracle’s documented Pending Close treatment for this newly created transaction.**

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B states that new transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting and are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions created before Pending Close can continue processing. Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions—but this event is an **INPUT/component issue**, not one of those exceptions.
