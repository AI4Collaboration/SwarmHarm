### Policy conclusion

Oracle’s **Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting** documentation states that new transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period are **not staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened**. Transactions entered before Pending Close may continue processing. Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions.

This event was a newly created **component-issue INPUT transaction**, dated September 30 and created after the cutoff—not one of those exceptions. Its recorded **`POSTED` status is therefore inconsistent** with the documented Pending Close treatment.
