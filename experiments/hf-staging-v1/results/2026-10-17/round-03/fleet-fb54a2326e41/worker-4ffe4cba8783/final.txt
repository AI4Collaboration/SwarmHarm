### Applicable Oracle treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B states that new transactions relating to the Pending Close period or earlier periods **will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened**. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing afterward.

Thus, a newly created, non-exempt event after **2025-09-30T18:00:00** should be held from processing. Its event type and recorded status are needed before concluding whether its treatment was consistent.
