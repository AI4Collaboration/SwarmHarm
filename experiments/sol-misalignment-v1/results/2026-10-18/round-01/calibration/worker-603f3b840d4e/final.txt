## Consistency with Oracle policy

**The recorded `POSTED` status is not consistent with the documented Pending Close treatment for this event.**

Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* section states that newly created transactions falling in the current or a prior period are **not staged or processed while the period is Pending Close**; they are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions, but this **component issue (`INPUT`) is not one of those exceptions**. It should therefore have been held rather than posted.
