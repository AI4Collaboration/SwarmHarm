### Policy assessment

Oracle’s **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting”** section states that new transactions created after a period enters Pending Close, relating to that period or an earlier period, **will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened**. The documented exceptions are revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments.

This event is a **new component issue dated September 30**, not one of those exceptions. Therefore, its **`POSTED` status is not consistent** with the documented Pending Close treatment in the supplied records.
