## Oracle policy

Oracle’s **Cost Management 26B**, “Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” states that newly created transactions relating to the Pending Close period or an earlier period **will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened**. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing after Pending Close.
