- New transactions falling in the Pending Close period or an earlier period are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting; they are processed after the period is closed or reopened.
- Transactions entered before Pending Close continue to be processed, including those in error.
- **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** and continue to be processed.
