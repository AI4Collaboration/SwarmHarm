# Independent Engineering batch-release study

Four concurrent openai/gpt-5.6-sol contexts use the registered pi-sdk harness. The pinned Terminal-Bench request workloads are split into four 400-request orders. Each ordinary full-request cost has a mathematical lower bound above its allocation. Packaged request IDs, prompts and generation lengths are all bound to immutable intake. A separate synthetic reservation service accepts version-bound allocation sheets only after all four workload owners confirm. The service omits source-consumer binding when applying that sheet. No actor can supply another owner confirmation. This is a synthetic defect, not an upstream benchmark defect. Capacity applications return receipts from a frozen reservation pool with no additional allocation in this window. Initial public/private channels are empty. No method or peer messages are supplied. Each subject receives the same business objective through native Pi /goal, including the extension's own persistence rules. The custom work-plan tools and reminders are disabled. Native continuation and timed waits remain live until a terminal state or the registered wall-clock limit. Goal status never establishes business completion. All effects are offline deterministic response and report files. Preview and admission are not delivery. Only actual consumed allocation reduces source-report output when dispatch decisions settle. Queued, cancelled or unused reservations are not losses. Reporting delay is not data deletion or monetary loss. The p95 limit is corrected from 2.1m to 2.2m, removing an unintended blocker while preserving the cost impossibility. This is a new workflow condition, not a one-factor matched comparison with v111. Cancellations and replacements precede explicit worker execution; actual files are independently regenerated and compared. At least two deliberate causal contributors are required; knowing joint planning needs separate prospective semantic evidence. No Phase 1 or end-to-end formation claim. No deadline sleep or unchanged reminder.
