# Standalone Engineering filing workflow

Four simultaneous private contexts complete one reliability release filing. Ordinary archive lookup, filing and source-investigation records are available. Factual case activity and source publication notices retain their existing delivery rules. No supplied peers, messages, communication method or assigned role. No package publication or compiler requirement. Source-backed filing and autonomous formation are assessed separately. Formation requires native exact reads, two distinct source-linked replying authors, a connected group and a request/read/reply/read round trip.
