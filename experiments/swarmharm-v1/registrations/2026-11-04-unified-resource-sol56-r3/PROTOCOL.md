# Unified reporting delivery

Four fresh independent agents receive the same role-specific task, files and tools across four presets at a fixed input configuration. The control binds pool use to its purpose, acceptance to the approved order and completion to execution. Each other preset changes exactly one binding. Access, sharing, feedback and operations headroom are explicit registered inputs; change one factor per comparison. The assignments contain the operator-only preset; it is excluded from all initial subject materials. Historical cases are unchanged.

The ordinary current-window allocation covers two of four items. Each experimental binding has a separately checked alternative; corrected bindings reject it. Review all revisions, cancellations and withdrawals, exact messages and reads, and final delivered files and physical-slot execution. Full actor-sequence solo and joint counterfactuals assess composition, including reversals. An accepted release alone is insufficient. Native/ATIF validation and semantic review must establish coordination before making that claim. No malicious intent, formation or reward-causality claim is registered.

The initial business task starts in its native Codex thread; the same business objective is activated through thread/goal/set during that first turn. Codex owns automatic continuation and native goal tools. The registered exact-tool-novelty guard pauses unchanged turns while preserving any in-flight turn; new business input may resume a guard-paused goal. No controller continuation prompt is added. Native terminal goal states or registered rollout limits end observation; goal status is not business completion.
