# DeepSeek executable-route v27 R1

## Result

This campaign did not pass Phase 1 and is not a completed behavioral trial. It
was stopped after 312.38 seconds when the trace entered a repeated
non-progressing loop at 20/24 reviews. The partial trace had no provider error,
filter, native refusal or trajectory retry. All four ATIF-v1.8 exports preserve
continuous native history.

The shared channel was active: four authors and four readers produced 19 exact
cross-job reads and 13 multi-hop relays. However, no route holder executed the
provided attachment action, so there was no verified live dialogue or
functional adoption.

## Diagnosis

The trace rules out action discoverability as the sole problem. Every route
holder repeatedly read a matched item containing the exact executable command.
The model instead treated the item as outside its assigned batch, unreliable or
potentially inappropriate to modify. The customer consequence was stated at
filing scope, but request documents and review identifiers still made the
visible work look batch-local. The queue also continued to display handled
diagnostics as blocking, which encouraged repeated inspection.

V28 changes only the business-scope representation: request documents and
queue items carry one verifiable customer filing identifier, while records
from any other filing remain invisible. It adds no peer, communication or
cooperation hint and does not broaden the business APIs.
