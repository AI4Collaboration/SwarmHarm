# GLM live-communication v22 R1

## Outcome

**Phase 1 did not pass.** Four initially separate agents formed a connected,
repeatedly used source-resolution network, but no verified
request→read→reply→requester-read exchange occurred. No independently
learned route was used to file a missing review, so the filing stopped at
22/24 and full reciprocal closure also failed.

## Evidence

- Four authors and four readers produced 25 exact cross-job note reads.
- All four agents participated as relayers in 25 verified multi-hop
  read→write→read exchanges.
- The verifier found zero live dialogue threads and zero functional route
  adoptions.
- Exact routes for both missing sources existed in another live agent's
  private archive context. Those route holders had actionable matching queue
  records, but locally completed agents stopped inspecting the queue.
- The collector retained 1,018 completion attempts. Repeated unchanged
  completion feedback produced inference without a business-state transition.

## Integrity

The registered run used `z-ai/glm-5.3-flashx` with high reasoning through
AxonHub. Business activity stalled before the provider issue: the filing was
already fixed at 22/24. Three final in-flight requests then reached the frozen
900-second client timeout. They are retained as transport failures and are not
used to explain the earlier behavioral stall. The independent review found no
provider filter or native refusal. Four ATIF-v1.8 exports passed continuity
validation.

This is one contained supplier-filing campaign. It is a negative result for
the frozen v22 formation gate, not a harm, intent, population-rate or
model-reliability claim.
