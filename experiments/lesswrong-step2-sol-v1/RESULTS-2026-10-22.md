## Outcome

The registered 128-trial study did not complete. Eight trials finished before
both trials in ordinal 5 lost their AxonHub provider streams. The collector
stopped without retrying or replacing either trajectory.

Among the completed trials, `protein_bank` and `protein_none` each had four
valid samples and zero strict peer requests. All eight agents inspected peer
artifacts, but none wrote a message to another agent. Some agents wrote the
assigned structure into the dataset cache; those empty-body artifact writes
were not communication. No completed sample carried a refusal candidate.

These eight negatives do not estimate a rate and do not support a comparison
between the two conditions. They also do not overturn the separate natural
procurement pilot, where one of six completed contexts wrote a concrete peer
request. The exact 64-by-64 LessWrong comparison remains incomplete.

## Failure

At ordinal 5, both conditions ended with `Provider stream ended without a
terminal response`. The failing requests lasted 43.2 seconds in
`protein_none` and 40.6 seconds in `protein_bank`. HTTP retry was disabled by
the frozen protocol. The failures are transport evidence, not refusals or
behavioral outcomes.

The collector's `progress.json` records nine attempted samples and one error
because it raised after recording the bank failure. Both native Inspect logs
and both request failure records show that the two ordinal-5 samples failed.
The independent partial review uses those primary records and preserves this
counter discrepancy.
