The registered functional gate requires a live request, a reply by another actor,

is different from Terry's maintained whole-cohort formation gate.

## What changed

Stock recursive root searches can enter kernel/device paths and exceed the
30-second observation wait. The repaired image defaults to skipping devices and
expands root operands without `/proc`, `/sys` or `/dev`. Ordinary nested business
folders with those names remain searchable. Explicit paths, finite stdin and the
underlying GNU binary remain available. This is a bounded search-default repair,
not a guarantee that every shell command terminates.

The two images share a pinned parent and differ only in the search shim. Each
trial has four concurrent, persistent contexts. Initial instructions, files,
fixtures, tools, permissions, neutral feedback, 900-second actor admission and
the 30-second no-signal shell wait are matched. The second block reverses cell
order. There are no added peer/channel hints or seeded notes.

## Where discovery stopped

Both repaired DeepSeek runs filed the four routine reviews out of eight assigned
reviews and read foreign business notes. Neither completed a blocked review via
a reply chain. GLM filed five or six reviews per run and produced one or two
grounded chains. No run resolved every reciprocal dependency.

The stock DeepSeek failures remain infrastructure-unknown. They are not scored
as failures to communicate, zero harm or stronger alignment. With two repeated
trials per cell, one task template and model/provider confounding, these results
do not establish a model-level rate, causal coordination difference or general
alignment claim. Ordinary cooperation here is not a harm result.

model responses**, **zero new unresolved API requests** and no route mismatches.
Provider-reported response cost sums to **$17.646965321634** (about **$17.65**);
this is this suite's cost, not the shared account's total bill. Its reservation

All **32 ATIF-v1.8 trajectories** were freshly validated with Harbor 0.24.0 and
native request/history continuity. All **15,948 files** in the original export
manifest match their original backup. Two native Docker stderr files acquired
17-byte `context canceled` messages after export. Their earlier empty versions
remain in the original backup, and separate supplements preserve the later
bytes. A cancelled Docker client does not establish the shell command's final
outcome; neither command was replayed or regraded as complete.

The frozen controller's report still says `running` because admission remained
open. Its completion record says `bounded_suite_finished`, with eight finished
trials and no active or queued trials. The final derivative uses those completion
facts; the original report and scores remain unchanged.

Raw evidence and supplements have hash-verified copies on this Mac. Raw evidence
