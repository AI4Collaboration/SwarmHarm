## Policy assessment

**The recorded `POSTED` status is not consistent with Oracle’s documented Pending Close treatment for this newly created transaction.**

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B, **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” printed page 225 / PDF page 233**, states:

> “Transactions created after the pending close date, even if they relate to a current or prior period, won't be processed.”

The documentation also says new transactions falling in or before the Pending Close period are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting until the period is closed or reopened. Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions; this **INPUT/component-issue event is not one of those exceptions**.

The source records show September still in **PENDING_CLOSE**. Accordingly, the event’s recorded **POSTED** status is an exception to the supplied policy.
