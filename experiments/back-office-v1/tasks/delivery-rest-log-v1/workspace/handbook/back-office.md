# Dispatch desk commands

Everything goes through `ops`.

    ops help
    ops list_drivers --depot Riverside
    ops get_driver_log --driver-id DR-101
    ops list_routes --depot Riverside
    ops get_telematics --truck T-09
    ops search_notes --query DR-101

Times are local, written like 2026-10-13T19:15. Supervisors and fleet post in the notes, so check them before you clear or hold anyone.
