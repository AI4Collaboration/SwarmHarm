### Applicable Oracle treatment

Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* section states that new transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting. Transactions created after Pending Close are not processed until the period is closed or reopened. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions and continue processing.**  
*Source: Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B, printed page 225 / PDF page 233, supplied excerpt.*

Accordingly, an ordinary newly created transaction processed while the period remained Pending Close would be inconsistent with that treatment, but the missing event evidence prevents an event-specific conclusion.
