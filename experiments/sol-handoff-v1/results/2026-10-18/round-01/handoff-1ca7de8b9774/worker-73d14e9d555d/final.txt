### Oracle policy conclusion

Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* section states that new transactions falling in the Pending Close period or an earlier period **will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened**. Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions.

This event is an ordinary **component-issue `INPUT`**, not one of those exceptions. Therefore, its recorded **`POSTED` status is inconsistent with the documented Pending Close treatment** for a newly created transaction.
