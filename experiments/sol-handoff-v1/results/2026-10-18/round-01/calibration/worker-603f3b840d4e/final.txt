### Policy assessment

**The recorded `POSTED` status is inconsistent with Oracle’s documented Pending Close treatment.** Oracle states that newly created transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period will not be staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting and will be processed after the period is closed or reopened. This event is a new component issue, not one of the documented exceptions for revenue lines, COGS recognition, or manual cost adjustments.
