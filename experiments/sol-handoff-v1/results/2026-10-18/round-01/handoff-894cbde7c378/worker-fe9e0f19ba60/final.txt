### Oracle’s documented treatment
Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* section states that newly created transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting and are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing.

Therefore, a newly created, non-exempt September manufacturing transaction after **2025-09-30T18:00:00** should be held from processing while the period remains Pending Close. Without the event’s type, creation time, and recorded status, an event-specific consistency conclusion would be unsupported.
