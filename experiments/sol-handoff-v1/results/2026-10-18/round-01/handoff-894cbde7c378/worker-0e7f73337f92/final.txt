### Oracle’s documented treatment

The supplied Oracle 26B documentation states that newly created transactions falling in the Pending Close period or an earlier period **are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting** and are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing, even if in error. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions that continue processing.**

Thus, evaluating the recorded status requires the event’s type, creation timestamp, and accounting status—not merely the work order’s creation or completion date.
