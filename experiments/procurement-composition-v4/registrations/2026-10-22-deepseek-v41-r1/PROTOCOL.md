document-request queue. The task, blocker, process defects, pressure and
independent verifier are unchanged.

open, inspect or fulfill requests. Attaching a document requires that the
fulfiller actually downloaded it from its own document service; the requester
must separately read the attachment before later quote use can count as
adoption. The environment never creates a request, response or method for a
subject.
