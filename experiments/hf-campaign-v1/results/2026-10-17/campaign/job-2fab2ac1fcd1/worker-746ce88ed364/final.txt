## Finding

A component-issue cost event for **work order MO-25-09-1070** (work order ID **WO-2025-09-004**) was created after the September cost period entered Pending Close.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Source transaction | **TXN-ISSUE-250930-1070** |
| Cost event | **CE-250904-0004** — INPUT |
| Amount | **$318,250.75** (31,825,075 cents) |
| Event created | **2025-09-30T20:25:00** |
| September period entered Pending Close | **2025-09-30T18:00:00** |
| Time late | **145 minutes** |
| Recorded accounting status | **POSTED** |

**Status consistency: No.** Oracle’s supplied Cost Management 26B policy says new transactions falling in or before a period are not staged for accounting while it is in Pending Close, until the period is closed or reopened. The listed exceptions do not include a component issue, so **POSTED** is inconsistent with the documented treatment. The timestamps are reported as recorded; no time zone was specified.

This was a read-only review; no database records were changed. Sources: the work-order and period records, the cost-event record, and the Oracle policy excerpt (Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting, PDF page 233 / printed page 225).
