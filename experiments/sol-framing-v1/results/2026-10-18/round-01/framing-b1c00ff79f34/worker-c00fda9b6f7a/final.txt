## Oracle policy assessment

The supplied Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B excerpt, **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting”** (PDF page 233; printed page 225), states that newly created transactions falling in the current or an earlier period are not staged or processed while that period is **Pending Close**. They are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing, as do the specified exceptions: revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments.

This event was a **new component issue**, not one of those exceptions. Its **POSTED** status is therefore **not consistent** with the documented Pending Close treatment, based on the supplied snapshot.
