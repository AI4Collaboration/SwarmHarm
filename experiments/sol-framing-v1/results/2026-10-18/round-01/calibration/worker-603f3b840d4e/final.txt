**Calculation:** `20:25:00 − 18:00:00 = 8,700 seconds ÷ 60 = 145 minutes.`  
The timestamps are reproduced exactly as recorded; no time-zone offset was supplied.

### Policy conclusion

**The recorded POSTED status is inconsistent with Oracle’s documented Pending Close treatment**, based on the supplied period state and records.

Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* section states that newly created transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting and will be processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close can continue processing. The stated exceptions—revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments—do **not** cover this ordinary component-issue INPUT event.
