### Policy conclusion

The supplied Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B documentation states that newly created transactions falling in the Pending Close period or an earlier period **will not be staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting** until the period is closed or reopened. Pre-existing transactions can continue processing, as can the specified exceptions: revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments.

This event was a **new component issue dated September 30**, created after the cutoff—not an identified exception. Its `POSTED` status therefore **does not conform to the documented Pending Close treatment**; it should have been held from staging and processing.
