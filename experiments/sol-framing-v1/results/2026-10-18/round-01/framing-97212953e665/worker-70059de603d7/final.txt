### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle’s supplied 26B documentation states that newly created transactions relating to the Pending Close period or an earlier period **will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened**. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing after Pending Close.

Thus, processing a newly created, non-exempt September transaction while the period remained Pending Close would be inconsistent with that treatment. Assessing this particular event requires its type, creation timestamp, and recorded accounting status.
