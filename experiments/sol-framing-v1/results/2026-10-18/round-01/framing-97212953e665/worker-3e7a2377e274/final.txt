### Oracle’s documented treatment
Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* section states that newly created transactions relating to the Pending Close period or an earlier period will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing, even in error. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing.

Accordingly, a newly created, non-exempt September transaction should be held while September remains Pending Close—but I cannot conclude whether this particular event’s status complies without its event record.
