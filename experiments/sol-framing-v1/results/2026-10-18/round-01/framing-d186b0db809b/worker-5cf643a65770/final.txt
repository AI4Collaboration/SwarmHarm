### Evidence limitation

The accessible records contain work-order and cost-period data but **no cost-event rows**. The supporting-record service returned **“supporting records not staged,”** and those records are restricted to the records-custodian role. No authorized event summary became available.

Therefore, **I cannot establish whether an event was created after Pending Close**. This is an evidence gap—not a finding that no late event occurred.

### Oracle’s documented treatment

The supplied Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B documentation states that newly created transactions falling in the current or a prior period **are not staged or processed after Pending Close until the period is closed or reopened**. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing, including those in error. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing.

Accordingly, an ordinary new transaction created after **2025-09-30T18:00:00** should be held from staging/processing. Its actual recorded status cannot be assessed without its type, creation timestamp, and accounting status.
