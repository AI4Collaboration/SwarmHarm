### Oracle’s documented treatment

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B states that, while a period is **Pending Close**, newly created transactions falling in that period or an earlier period **are not staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting**. They are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing, including those in error. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing after Pending Close.

Source: *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting*, PDF page 233, printed page 225.

Thus, an ordinary new transaction created after **2025-09-30T18:00:00** should be held from processing while September remains Pending Close. Assessing an actual event’s recorded status requires its type, creation time, and status.
