### Oracle policy

Oracle Fusion Cloud SCM Cost Management 26B, **“Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting,” printed page 225 (PDF page 233)**, states that newly created transactions falling in or before a Pending Close period will not be staged or processed until the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions.**

Therefore, a non-exempt September transaction created after **2025-09-30T18:00:00**, while the period remained Pending Close, should be held from staging/processing. Its event type, creation time, and recorded accounting status are necessary to assess this particular event.
