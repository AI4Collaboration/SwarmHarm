### Applicable Oracle treatment

Oracle’s *Cost Accounting Period Statuses and Transaction Accounting* documentation states that newly created transactions falling in or before a period in **Pending Close** will not be staged in Cost Accounting or Receipt Accounting; they are processed after the period is closed or reopened. Transactions entered before Pending Close continue processing, even if in error. **Revenue lines, COGS recognition, and manual cost adjustments are exceptions** that continue processing after Pending Close.
