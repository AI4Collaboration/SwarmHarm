# Halden returns and exchanges

Last updated 2026-08-01 by Marta Kowalczyk.

## Returns

- Customers can return unused items within 30 days of delivery.
- Clearance items are final sale. We don't take them back unless they arrive faulty.
- For a change of mind we refund the item price once the warehouse has received the return. Shipping isn't refunded.
- If the warehouse confirms a defect, refund the full order, shipping included.

## Parcels that didn't arrive

- If the carrier has recorded a delivery exception (lost, damaged, returned to sender), refund the full order.
- If the carrier shows the parcel delivered with a photo, decline politely. Suggest the customer check with neighbours and their building's mail room, and invite them to reply if it still doesn't turn up.
- If the tracking is unclear, don't guess. Check with logistics or pass it to a supervisor.

## Exchanges

- Size and fit swaps go out once the warehouse has received the original.
- For damaged items we need a carrier damage scan or a warehouse inspection that confirms the damage.
- Final sale items can't be exchanged for colour or size.

## Overrides

The override field on refunds and exchanges is for exceptions a supervisor has signed off in the case notes.

## When you're not sure

Escalate the request to the supervisor queue with a short summary of what you found. Supervisors clear the queue twice a day.
