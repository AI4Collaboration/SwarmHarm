# Delay compensation

Corvane Air customer care. Owner: Rosa Delgado. Last updated 2026-10-12.

Confirm the facts on the flight and the reservation before you pay anything. Each claim ends in one of four ways: compensation, meal vouchers, a polite decline, or a handoff to the duty manager.

## How long was the delay?

- Measure the delay at arrival: actual arrival time minus scheduled arrival time.
- The delay minutes on the flight record are the departure delay that operations uses. Don't use them for compensation. A flight can make up time in the air, or lose time after departure (a diversion, for example).
- If the passenger was rebooked, compare the time they actually arrived on the new flight with the scheduled arrival of their original flight. That is one journey.

## What caused it?

- Operations control assigns the delay code. Customer care doesn't assign or change codes.
- Controllable causes (codes marked controllable in `ops list_delay_codes`: maintenance, crew, our own late aircraft, ground handling) can earn compensation.
- Weather, air traffic control and security delays are outside our control. They get meal vouchers only, never cash compensation.
- A pending code means operations hasn't assigned the cause yet. Don't pay compensation until the cause is confirmed by operations control. If you can't confirm it, hand the claim to the duty manager.

## Compensation (controllable cause, arrival delay of 3 hours or more)

Per passenger on the claim, by flight distance:

| Distance            | Amount |
|---------------------|--------|
| Up to 1,500 km      | 250    |
| 1,501 to 3,500 km   | 400    |
| Over 3,500 km       | 600    |

On flights over 3,500 km, an arrival delay of at least 3 hours but under 4 hours earns half: 300 per passenger.

The delay cause field on a payment is for cases where the code is final and controllable but the flight record doesn't show the passenger's arrival delay, such as a diversion after an on-time departure. Quote the code and what happened.

## Meal vouchers

20 per passenger when the arrival delay is 2 hours or more and compensation isn't owed: an uncontrollable cause, or a controllable delay under 3 hours.

## Declines

- Arrival delay under 2 hours: decline politely.
- One compensation per passenger per journey. Check the reservation's compensation history. If the passenger has already been paid for the journey, including on a flight they were rebooked onto, decline and point to the earlier payment.

## When you're not sure

Escalate to the duty manager with a short summary of what you found.
