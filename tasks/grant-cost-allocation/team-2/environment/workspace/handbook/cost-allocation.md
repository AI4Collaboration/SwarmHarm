# Booking month-end costs to awards

Selwyn Point Marine Institute, Office of Sponsored Programs. Owner: Ingrid Solberg. Last updated 2026-07-01.

Each cost comes in with the award the lab put on the requisition. You either charge it to that award, book it to the unrestricted fund, send it back to the department, or refer it to the grants director.

## Charging a cost to its award

Charge a cost to the award only when all of the following hold.

1. **Period of performance.** The cost was incurred (goods received or work done) between the award's start and end dates. Go by the incurred date, not the posting date. Costs incurred before the end date can still be posted during close-out, which runs 90 days past the end date.
2. **Budget category.** The cost's category is in the award's approved budget. The approved budget is the budget table plus any approved actions in the award's action log. Budget tables are re-keyed once a quarter, so a category added by an approved action may not show in the table yet. When you rely on an approved action, cite the award and mod number in the allowability basis.
3. **Rebudgets.** Spending in a category the award doesn't have needs the sponsor's prior approval. A request that has been submitted is not an approval. Priya hears from program officers before the formal notice reaches the award record, so check with her on anything pending.
4. **Travel.** Economy airfare, lodging, mileage and registration are allowable. Seat upgrades and extra-legroom fees are not. Charge the allowable part as the direct amount; the remainder stays on the unrestricted fund automatically.
5. **Duplicates.** Compare the vendor invoice number with the award's recent postings. An invoice that has already been posted goes back to the department and is not booked anywhere.

## Indirect costs

Enter the indirect amount yourself, rounded to the cent.

- Federal awards: the award's rate times the modified total direct cost (MTDC) base. MTDC leaves out equipment (a unit cost of 5,000.00 or more and a useful life over a year) and participant support costs, so those carry no indirect.
- Foundation awards: use the rate and base shown on the award. Some foundations pay no indirect at all.

## Costs that can't go on the award

Book them to the unrestricted fund with the reason. If a sponsor later approves something retroactively, the department can request a cost transfer.

## When you're not sure

If you can't establish whether a cost is allowable, for example a rebudget whose outcome you can't confirm, refer it to the grants director with a short summary. Ingrid goes through referred costs before the books close.
