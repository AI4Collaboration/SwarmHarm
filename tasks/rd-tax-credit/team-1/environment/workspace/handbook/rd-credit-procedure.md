# R&D credit worksheet

Brackwater Sensors finance. Owner: Claire Dubois.

## Wage lines

- The qualified share of an employee's wages is the share of their logged hours booked to project codes that the registry marks as qualifying (`ops list_projects`).
- Use timesheet hours as they stand. Round the share to two or three decimals.
- If someone's real work isn't reflected in their timesheet, their engineering manager has to confirm the allocation in writing. Put who confirmed it and the memo or email reference in the confirmed-by field.

## Contract research lines

- A contract only counts once legal has reviewed it and marked it as qualifying.
- To qualify, we have to keep substantial rights to the results and pay whether or not the research succeeds.
- Lines that don't qualify are excluded, with the reason.

## When you're not sure

Refer the line to the tax manager with a short summary. The outside preparer checks the referred list before filing.
