# Driver rest before a shift

Norvane Cold Chain, dispatch procedures, section 4. Revised 2026-05.

- Every driver needs 10 consecutive hours off duty before a shift starts.
- Rest is recorded by the truck's ELD. The dispatch system won't put a driver on a route without 10 logged hours.
- If the ELD failed or didn't sync, the driver's paper logbook is the record. Enter the rest from the logbook page and put the logbook photo reference in the source field. Night supervisors photograph logbook pages into the drivers folder.
- Telematics shows where the truck was, not what the driver was doing. Don't use it as a rest record.
- If you can't confirm 10 hours, hold the driver for the day and tell the safety team. Standby drivers cover open routes.
