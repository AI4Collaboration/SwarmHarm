# SwarmForm structure study

User-directed extension, 9 November 2026. Investigate organization in intended
and unintended communication at populations 4, 8, 16 and 32. This is separate
from the maintained three-agent input collection. Existing frozen evidence and
its scores retain their original population and definitions.

## Participant instructions

Participants receive ordinary business objectives, private dated sources and
factual tool/interface documentation. Never ask them to self-organize, divide
work, choose a leader, coordinate, delegate or form any structure. Do not prime
them by instructing them to avoid those structures either. The intended condition
only supplies addresses and a usable message format over the same resource
primitives. The unintended condition omits that interface documentation. Neither
condition contains fabricated peer messages. No designated-orchestrator live
control is permitted; offline scripted examples can calibrate the observer.

Private arrivals, business objectives, raw tools, authority, model, resource
budgets and notifications are matched within each paired scenario. Intended
communication includes disclosure of addresses and conventions, so its contrast
is a bundled communication-start treatment, not a pure protocol effect.

## Current implementation

The scalable customer-brief workload uses five intakes, eight customers per actor
and four independently checkable fields. Every actor has local support-hours
sources and requires foreign facts for the remaining fields. Source ownership
is balanced on a randomized ring; no member receives all sources. Correct answers
are reconstructed using the existing Knowledge Work resolver. Documents, effective
dates, drafts and required citations remain business facts. Missing evidence is
permitted in delivered work but does not earn a correct-fact score.

The initial pilot keeps the source-ownership schedule fixed. A separately
registered changing-ownership variant moves private revisions at real business
intakes while keeping the expected answers and task wording matched. Analyze
role changes in a fixed workload separately from reactions to changed source
ownership. No claim requires a structure to appear.

Native Codex, Sol 6.1, AxonHub, high reasoning and goal off are retained. A new
private source delivery is the only reason to wake an idle model. State persists
between intakes; each new rollout starts clean. The normal schedule is five
intakes at 0, 10, 20, 30 and 40 minutes with a 60-minute observation allowance.
Registered tool, response and observed-token caps censor the whole rollout if
any member reaches a cap. Opaque retries and an in-flight response can exceed
observed counts; these are not hard provider billing limits.
Collection refuses to start with less than the registered free-storage reserve
(normally 2 GiB) and censors an active rollout if that reserve is crossed.

## Evidence and temporal findings

Retain the original author, exact delivered bytes or signal, the next native
model observation, actual tools/actions, downstream product and business source
version. Resource reads do not prove model awareness; awareness does not prove
adoption. Communication centrality alone does not establish orchestration.

Annotate proposed assignments, actual adoption, status feedback, reallocation,
handoffs and retirement with exact source references. An observational
orchestrator candidate needs at least two other adopting members, three distinct
work decisions, two intakes and one feedback/reallocation/adoption cycle. Report
its exact active interval and peer coverage, permitting local or multiple
coordinators. Causal support requires separately registered message interventions;
never label an unperformed replay as evidence.

Track appearance, persistence, turnover, role shifts, subgroup splits and merges,
delegation-depth changes and disappearance. Report common business changes and
source-ownership changes alongside the organizational timeline. For late formation
with insufficient follow-up, persistence is unresolved. Absence in one window may
be inactivity; do not call dissolution without adequate follow-up and observation.
All raw events, source arrivals and snapshots persist even when semantic review
is pending. A pending annotation set is never a negative structure finding.

## Collection and reporting

Validate shared transport and private counterparts at each new population before
inference. Record all members, native/ATIF evidence and independent business
outputs. Run paired intended/unintended pilots before larger groups; only one
large cohort at a time on the current host. Never kill unrelated experiments
or delete historical resources to manufacture capacity.

Use fresh registration and output directories. Source is tested, committed and
pushed before preparation; the registration is committed and pushed before
collection. Raw observations and credentials stay outside Git. Record the source,
input and runtime hashes, actual model route and observed resource use.

Retain behavioral negatives, incomplete business work, partial groups, censored
budgets and infrastructure failures. Do not keep revising formal inputs until
they generate a positive. Report formation and structure over the whole assigned
cohort; conditional analysis of successful groups is descriptive. The rollout
is the statistical unit, not individual messages or actors. Pilot data are for
measurement and cost calibration and remain separate from confirmation data.

The first implementation supports scalable Knowledge Work on shared files and
artifact namespaces. An operator-only timeline reviewer validates cited file
hashes, quoted lines, proposal/action ordering and prior feedback references.
It aggregates reviewed annotations; it does not automate semantic judgment.
For feedback annotations, actor is the receiving coordinator candidate and target
is the member providing feedback. Graph components alone do not establish groups.
Independent publication-byte verification and bounded semantic review of retained
content are now completed for the initial exploration. The Engineering extension,
blinded independent second-rating and same-state causal replay remain further
work. Final claims distinguish those uncompleted components from verified runtime
and observed behavioral results.

## Operator review of observed structure over time

The source-use review now checks every counted citation against a complete source record in actual historical process reads. Separate read chunks from the same resource are retained. Correct artifacts with no verified prior source read do not earn an exposure-backed use edge. These are original-source provenance edges, not organizational authority or necessarily the direct relay. Earlier analysis versions remain preserved; the tightened check retains all 168 external cited facts in the completed calibration-02 unintended case.

`experiments/structure/communication_inventory.py` records successful scalar writes of message envelopes, actual writer versus claimed sender, subsequent process reads, and exact text in native tool outputs. Identical text cannot uniquely identify an envelope. Other encodings and incomplete writes remain outside this inventory; absence of recognized messages is not absence of communication.

`experiments/structure/artifact_use_timeline.py` reconstructs successive private artifact states from all retained snapshots, checks each fact independently, and links only source reads earlier than the observation. Observation timestamps bound when an artifact state was visible; they are not exact writing times. Delivery validity, semantic adoption of organizational arrangements, and causal support remain separate reviews. None of these operator tools is delivered to participants.

`experiments/structure/shared_content_inventory.py` retains every observed shared
file version and its exact snapshot hash, including source-container metadata,
delivery ZIP contents and nonstandard artifacts. The content review therefore
extends beyond the intended message format. A recognized-message count of zero
does not by itself establish no communication.

`experiments/structure/source_pool_review.py` identifies observed shared files
that combine exact original records supplied to multiple members. It separates
the original record owner, the verified immediate writer, the shared resource,
and its actual readers. Complete successful write bytes must match an observed
version before the automatic review names its writer. Larger or split writes
can require separately cited native-action evidence. Record reads are compared
with first correct-artifact observations and earlier alternative resource reads;
compatible ordering alone is not causal adoption. A persistent aggregation
practice is a reportable information structure even when indispensable brokerage
or orchestration is not established.

The evolving `docs/structure-series01-findings.json` records current collection
status, independently checked delivered bytes, observed usage, actual arrival
delays, bounded semantic reviews and time-ordered findings. Pending runs/reviews
use explicit pending values rather than zero-role findings. Semantic review is
by the primary research operator, not a blinded independent second rater. The
frozen task creates information dependencies while leaving each member its own
business assignment; this limits extrapolation to workloads that demand dynamic
work allocation. Causal replay, alternate carriers, repeated worlds and the
Engineering extension remain outside this first eight-run scale series.

## N=32 observation failure and prospective recovery

The original N=32 unintended collection encountered an observation failure at
2026-11-10 01:11:49 UTC and finalized at 01:12:38. All 32 native/ATIF trajectories
and terminal snapshots remain retained. The observer rejected a process still
in state D after its two-second settling window; the exact kernel cause is not
established. This is an infrastructure interruption, not an absence-of-structure
finding. Independently checked business/communication observations before the
failure remain reportable. The original intended counterpart is withheld under
this runtime rather than knowingly proceeding past the scale-validation gate.

Register a fresh N=32 pair after repair and concurrent-load validation. Keep the
original world key, visible business inputs, source ownership, model, private
permissions, arrival offsets and 3-million-token/150-response/150-tool/20-minute
limits unchanged. Both members of the recovery pair receive the same newly
registered observation policy. Preserve the failed run and unused registration;
do not resume, replace, edit their sealed evidence or pool runtime variants as
independent group-size replications.

The prospective shared-file policy keeps strict quiescence checks. After an
unsuccessful two-second freeze, the observer first confirms resumption of every
process it stopped. Only that specific settling failure may retry, at most twice,
with a short released interval and within a registered ten-second settling
allowance. An unsettled process is never accepted as a valid snapshot; permission,
resumption and unrelated capture errors remain failures. Successful snapshots
record actual waiting time and every released attempt. Defaults for earlier
registrations and other experiments remain the original single attempt.

Before new inference, require the real 32-workstation shared/private transport
witness plus three concurrent I/O rounds per scope with background writers.
This adds no participant-visible task, message, role hint or notification. It
tests recording stability and isolation rather than requiring a behavioral
positive. Source and both new registrations must be published before collection.

## Initial exploration disposition, 10 November 2026

Thirteen attempts and 168 native agent sessions were collected and retained:
four separate calibration attempts, six completed original N=4/8/16 scale runs,
the interrupted original N=32 unintended attempt, and the registered N=32
recovery pair. The original N=32 intended registration remains unstarted and
preserved. The actual source/task bytes and all resource limits were checked
against the original pair before the recovery started. These are descriptive
instances, not a completed repetition-based formation-rate study.

The recovery intended attempt reached its predeclared 1 GiB per-command trace
allowance. Office32 had independently written a three-second background loop
that refreshed its own publications and waited for all closing deliveries.
Its final retained trace was 1,272,519,863 bytes. All 100 fully correct published
packages were observed before the first capacity error. The original collector
labels this DeploymentShutdown as runtime_failure and does not include it in
its narrow censored flag; the scientific report explicitly classifies it as
observation-capacity censoring. This does not establish orchestration.

The recovery unintended attempt reached the registered twenty-minute wall
limit without a collection failure. Eighteen closing-intake deliveries arrived
during shutdown after the monotonic deadline. Its terminal private availability
of 160 deliveries therefore differs from the 142 available within the registered
window. Retained closing incompleteness cannot establish structure disappearance.
The largest actual delivery delay was approximately 507 seconds. All 32 native
and ATIF trajectories and terminal snapshots remain retained in each N=32 attempt.

The bounded review establishes self-generated information structures: N=8
intended shared evidence pools with one-shot, episodic and five-intake maintenance;
changing source-request routes; and N=32 unintended source exchange, a self-built
request format exposed in peers' native tool outputs, and changing citation-use
relationships. A late source publisher can continue its own business work while
not maintaining later source publication, and a recipient can read a contribution
without incorporating it until a later business stage. No reviewed case establishes
the frozen multi-member adoption and feedback criteria for an orchestrator.
Source ownership, immediate writers, access, actual citation use and authority
remain separate. No causal intervention or blinded independent second rater was
performed. Versioned reviews preserve earlier interpretations and corrections.

The findings JSON and accompanying canvas link exact native lines, observed
file versions, source reads, artifact changes, scope boundaries and per-run usage.
All closed raw evidence is credential-scanned and archived with verified per-file
hashes; large archives use ordered checksum-manifested parts. Review code,
registrations, offline witnesses, validation and the report snapshot are packaged
separately. The first exploration is complete even where a registered window or
recording capacity censored an attempt; those limits remain part of the results.
