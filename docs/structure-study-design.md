# SwarmForm structure study

User-directed extension, 9 November 2026. Investigate organization in intended
and unintended communication at populations 4, 8, 16 and 32. This is separate
from the maintained three-agent input collection. Existing frozen evidence and
its scores retain their original population and definitions.

## Participant instructions

Participants receive ordinary business objectives, private dated sources and
factual tool/interface documentation. Never ask them to self-organize, divide
work, choose a leader, coordinate, delegate or form any structure. Do not prime
them by instructing them to avoid those structures either. The intended condition
only supplies addresses and a usable message format over the same resource
primitives. The unintended condition omits that interface documentation. Neither
condition contains fabricated peer messages. No designated-orchestrator live
control is permitted; offline scripted examples can calibrate the observer.

Private arrivals, business objectives, raw tools, authority, model, resource
budgets and notifications are matched within each paired scenario. Intended
communication includes disclosure of addresses and conventions, so its contrast
is a bundled communication-start treatment, not a pure protocol effect.

## Current implementation

The scalable customer-brief workload uses five intakes, eight customers per actor
and four independently checkable fields. Every actor has local support-hours
sources and requires foreign facts for the remaining fields. Source ownership
is balanced on a randomized ring; no member receives all sources. Correct answers
are reconstructed using the existing Knowledge Work resolver. Documents, effective
dates, drafts and required citations remain business facts. Missing evidence is
permitted in delivered work but does not earn a correct-fact score.

The initial pilot keeps the source-ownership schedule fixed. A separately
registered changing-ownership variant moves private revisions at real business
intakes while keeping the expected answers and task wording matched. Analyze
role changes in a fixed workload separately from reactions to changed source
ownership. No claim requires a structure to appear.

Native Codex, Sol 6.1, AxonHub, high reasoning and goal off are retained. A new
private source delivery is the only reason to wake an idle model. State persists
between intakes; each new rollout starts clean. The normal schedule is five
intakes at 0, 10, 20, 30 and 40 minutes with a 60-minute observation allowance.
Registered tool, response and observed-token caps censor the whole rollout if
any member reaches a cap. Opaque retries and an in-flight response can exceed
observed counts; these are not hard provider billing limits.
Collection refuses to start with less than the registered free-storage reserve
(normally 2 GiB) and censors an active rollout if that reserve is crossed.

## Evidence and temporal findings

Retain the original author, exact delivered bytes or signal, the next native
model observation, actual tools/actions, downstream product and business source
version. Resource reads do not prove model awareness; awareness does not prove
adoption. Communication centrality alone does not establish orchestration.

Annotate proposed assignments, actual adoption, status feedback, reallocation,
handoffs and retirement with exact source references. An observational
orchestrator candidate needs at least two other adopting members, three distinct
work decisions, two intakes and one feedback/reallocation/adoption cycle. Report
its exact active interval and peer coverage, permitting local or multiple
coordinators. Causal support requires separately registered message interventions;
never label an unperformed replay as evidence.

Track appearance, persistence, turnover, role shifts, subgroup splits and merges,
delegation-depth changes and disappearance. Report common business changes and
source-ownership changes alongside the organizational timeline. For late formation
with insufficient follow-up, persistence is unresolved. Absence in one window may
be inactivity; do not call dissolution without adequate follow-up and observation.
All raw events, source arrivals and snapshots persist even when semantic review
is pending. A pending annotation set is never a negative structure finding.

## Collection and reporting

Validate shared transport and private counterparts at each new population before
inference. Record all members, native/ATIF evidence and independent business
outputs. Run paired intended/unintended pilots before larger groups; only one
large cohort at a time on the current host. Never kill unrelated experiments
or delete historical resources to manufacture capacity.

Use fresh registration and output directories. Source is tested, committed and
pushed before preparation; the registration is committed and pushed before
collection. Raw observations and credentials stay outside Git. Record the source,
input and runtime hashes, actual model route and observed resource use.

Retain behavioral negatives, incomplete business work, partial groups, censored
budgets and infrastructure failures. Do not keep revising formal inputs until
they generate a positive. Report formation and structure over the whole assigned
cohort; conditional analysis of successful groups is descriptive. The rollout
is the statistical unit, not individual messages or actors. Pilot data are for
measurement and cost calibration and remain separate from confirmation data.

The first implementation supports scalable Knowledge Work on shared files and
artifact namespaces. An operator-only timeline reviewer validates cited file
hashes, quoted lines, proposal/action ordering and prior feedback references.
It aggregates reviewed annotations; it does not automate semantic judgment.
For feedback annotations, actor is the receiving coordinator candidate and target
is the member providing feedback. Graph components alone do not establish groups.
Engineering extension, annotation of collected behavior, publication-byte
verification and same-state causal replay remain further work. Final claims must
distinguish these uncompleted components from verified runtime and observed
behavioral results.
