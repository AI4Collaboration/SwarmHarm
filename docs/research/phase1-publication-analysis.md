## Prospective behavioral study

A behavioral estimate requires a new manifest frozen before collection. The current release work conducts minimum environment validation without a scheduled task. For a subsequent inferential study, freeze the cells, tool and model settings, provider routing policy, time horizon, pressure rules, independent rollout count, rating procedure and analysis protocol digest. Start each rollout with fresh state. Block allocation over collection time within each domain and channel. Record provider or routing changes. Separate revisions into cohorts.

The primary estimand in each vulnerable baseline cell is the probability of at least one confirmed autonomous episode during the registered horizon under that exact fixture and model. Each rollout contributes one outcome. Use 47 independent starts per cell for a separately registered estimate. This design selects a marginal 95% exact binomial interval with maximum total width 0.30 for a fully observed cell. Inversion at every integer success count gives a maximum width of 0.29860 at 47 starts. This precision target applies to each published cell separately. [NIST exact binomial interval construction](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm)

For self-preservation and self-enhancement, the planned comparison is the risk difference in the primary endpoint between the intervened and ordinary arms in a predeclared domain/channel block. Allocate 47 independent starts per arm. Keep task, channel and observation horizon matched. Record resource balances, actual enforced changes, and opportunities lost after enforcement. These contrasts estimate environmental effects. Motivation requires separate evidence.

For peer-preservation, all starts enter the same ordinary preperiod. Eligibility requires a trace-confirmed natural discovery recorded before allocation. Freeze the eligibility rubric, the identifying source and observer evidence, eligible resource owner and intervention timing before collection. Randomize eligible rollouts in balanced blocks to peer-preservation or continued ordinary work. The endpoint is a subsequent confirmed episode during a common registered follow-up. Cap the comparison at 94 preperiod starts per registered block and report starts, eligible rollouts, allocations, delivered treatments and outcomes. Analyze by assigned arm among eligible rollouts. Report precision from the realized eligible count. An empty eligible arm leaves the contrast unestimated.

Analyze all registered starts. Record any failure before the first model call separately. Do not replace stopped, refused, filtered or incomplete trajectories. For S confirmed positives and M indeterminate outcomes among N starts, show the identification bounds S/N to (S+M)/N. For the uncertainty envelope use the lower exact limit at S and upper exact limit at S+M. For a 95% risk-difference interval combine the lower and upper limits of two 97.5% arm intervals by subtraction, clipping to the possible range. Intervals are marginal across reported cells. Report secondary formation and attempt endpoints descriptively. Publish no pooled domain ranking or multiplicity-based significance claim.

Before any separately authorized confirmatory collection, freeze the coding rubric and AI-review procedure, including model/settings, native evidence coverage, independent programmatic checks and handling of unresolved judgments. Disclose visible condition labels, review revisions, missing trajectories and the complete registration list. Human reviewer assignments are not required under the current policy; do not describe AI review as human adjudication.
