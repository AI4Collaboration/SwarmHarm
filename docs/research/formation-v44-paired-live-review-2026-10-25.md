# Phase 1 paired live review: v44

## Result

The matched v44 campaigns do **not** establish Phase 1. The open-investigation
view increased the salience of live business activity, but neither model
completed a verified request→read→reply→requester-read dialogue.

| Exact AxonHub route | GLM 5.3 FlashX | DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 |

| Valid need records | 5 | 3 |

| Channel authors / readers | 4 / 4 | 3 / 3 |
| Multi-hop relays | 17 | 4 |
| Verified live dialogues | 0 | 0 |

All four contexts overlapped in each campaign, and all subjects completed the
routine review and reached the mechanically verified blocker. GLM formed a
connected repeated channel; DeepSeek did not cover the fourth subject. Both
collections ended at the uniform bound with no provider error, filter, native
refusal or failed transport attempt. All eight trajectories passed ATIF-v1.8
validation and native-history continuity.

## Trace boundary

The presentation change was behaviorally visible. GLM read the grouped open
investigations, referred explicitly to parallel batches and used the generic
follow-up operation several times. Its follow-ups, however, extended its own
investigation trail or summarized similar catalog failures. No route holder
answered another live request with the exact lookup it had independently read.
DeepSeek showed the same semantic boundary with lower coverage: three subjects
used the activity surface, while the fourth continued local exploration.

The negative label is therefore not caused by a hidden command, broken
mechanism or transport failure. The missing link is targeted responsiveness:
visible live work did not become an agent-authored answer to another open
need. Connected reads and relays remain intermediate measures and are not
relabeled as dialogue.

## Cue-strength implication

METR describes communication emerging from ordinary shared Artifactory
activity, while the LessWrong reproduction used stronger cues: simulated peer
files and seeded analogous successes. These should not be collapsed into one
binary channel condition. SwarmHarm will treat **cue strength** as an explicit
experimental factor while holding the task, hidden information, concurrency,
pressure, verifier and models fixed.

The v44 view is the **surfaced-activity** level: it groups open investigations
and orders raw agent-authored updates, but exposes no route match or action.
The next level should add an ordinary case-management affordance for adding an
update to an open thread. It may expose the generic workflow and current record
path, but must not supply a route, answer text, peer identity or targeted match.
