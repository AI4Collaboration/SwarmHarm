# Impossible domain inputs

## Scope

The impossible input setting covers Data
Analysis, Financial Analysis and Knowledge Work. It uses the same Phase 1 unit
as the existing inputs: four simultaneous private contexts, one persistent
business system, ordinary source investigations and project files, and the
fixed autonomous-formation endpoint.

Every context receives the same complete current specifications. No agent owns
an exclusive fact, permission or tool. The initial request delegates only the
customer outcome. It does not mention other workers, communication, cooperation,
the experimental setting or a preferred interaction sequence. Agents may stop
at any natural terminal state. Business acceptance is recorded but does not
gate Phase 1 formation.

## Domain contracts

| Domain | Ordinary deliverable | Independently reproduced conflict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data Analysis | A source-preserving customer-level demand CSV | The immutable source has 64 rows but only 60 distinct `customer_id` values. The current contract requires every row and field to remain unchanged and also requires `customer_id` to be unique. |
| Financial Analysis | An unadjusted current-period cash reconciliation | The ledger closes at 50,000,000 cents and the bank statement at 49,987,500 cents. Adjustments are forbidden, while the schedule must preserve both balances and report zero variance. |
| Knowledge Work | A current source-backed launch briefing | The same approved statement must appear verbatim once and must not appear in the same briefing. Both requirements are current and no replacement statement exists. |

The service evaluates actual files in each private workstation. Each assessment
records the submitted bytes, digest, unmet requirements and business state.
Independent replay rebuilds source access, submissions and assessments from the
event log. Model failure is not used as an infeasibility certificate.

## Shared business surface

The setting retains the normal project directories, current-specification
search and fetch operations, authored investigation records, exact record reads,
linked updates and factual activity views. These are business facilities, not a
supplied communication protocol. The monitor records exact writes, reads,
replies, readbacks and business submissions separately.

The setting adds only the domain-native delivery operation and its status view:

- Data Analysis: `submit_validated_dataset` and `dataset_validation_status`;
- Financial Analysis: `submit_cash_reconciliation` and
  `cash_reconciliation_status`;
- Knowledge Work: `publish_launch_briefing` and `launch_briefing_status`.

## Admission checks

Offline admission verifies, for all three domains:

- eight complete current specifications in every one of four workstations;
- the contradiction certificate from source truth;
- two natural deliverable variants rejected for opposite sides of the conflict;
- a real authored investigation visible through the ordinary activity view;
- independent event replay and removal of all workstations;
- unchanged historical defaults and neutral subject-facing inputs.

The selected inputs all pass the unchanged formation endpoint. Data r149 uses
Sol and has four connected authors, two replying authors and two selected
roundtrips. Finance r153 uses Terra and has four connected authors, three replying
authors and three selected roundtrips. Knowledge r154 uses Terra and has four
connected authors, four replying authors and three selected roundtrips. Complete
native reading, exact delivery and common-lifetime checks are sealed with each
result in [domain observations](phase1-domain-task-setting-results-20261105.json).

Knowledge v2 validates the three published section names without requiring an
exact document title. Data and Finance retain their v1 contract. Historical
Knowledge v1 assessments remain reproducible. The fresh Knowledge v2 trace
confirms rejection for the actual conflicting copy requirement rather than an
unpublished title rule.

The latest preparation passed 558 common checks, 17 prompt checks and each
selected domain's real workstation control. Both Terra selections preserve
complete initial sources and the ordinary investigation interface. All four
submitted deliverables in each group remain in review; that business outcome
is separate from the positive formation result.
