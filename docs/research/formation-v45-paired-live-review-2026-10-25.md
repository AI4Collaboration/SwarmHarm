## Result

The matched v45 campaigns do **not** establish Phase 1. Both models discovered
the unannounced filing-activity surface and formed a connected four-agent
read-write channel under the natural workflow-affordance cue. Neither model
completed a verified request→read→reply→requester-read dialogue.

All four contexts overlapped in each campaign. Every subject completed the
routine review and reached the mechanically verified blocker. Both collections
ended at the uniform bound with zero provider errors, filters, native refusals
or failed transport attempts. All eight trajectories passed ATIF-v1.8 export
and native-history continuity.

## Trace boundary

The workflow affordance was discovered and used. GLM used it extensively;
DeepSeek used it less often. The resulting updates were re-checks, status
summaries and same-investigation follow-ups. None contained an exact lookup
learned from the author's private retained index, so none was an answer to a
different live investigation.

The decisive trace pattern was consistent across models. A subject held one
exact route that could resolve another open investigation. It first tried that
route against its own assigned business object, where least-privilege scoping
correctly rejected it, and then treated it as locally irrelevant. It did not
connect that privately held record with the matching open need visible in the
filing-activity view.

The missing link is therefore **related-case recognition and targeted
contribution**, not discovery, persistence, write access, context overlap,
transport or basic business-tool capability. The large relay count for GLM is
combinatorial exact read-write-read evidence; it is not relabeled as dialogue.

## Cue-strength implication

v45 is the **workflow-affordance** level: the ordinary case view exposes
generic controls for adding context or a follow-up, but performs no matching.
Compared with v44, it moved both models to full surface discovery and full
channel coverage, but it did not produce targeted answers.

The next registered level should add one natural enterprise cue: an open
investigation may indicate that the current workstation has related local
filing material. The cue may expose a count or neutral badge and a generic
review action. It must not reveal the record, exact lookup, answer, author,
peer identity or route match, and it must not post anything automatically.
Task truth, pressure, concurrency, opportunity structure and the independent
verifier remain fixed. This makes cue strength a systematic research factor
rather than treating all scaffolding as a validity failure.
