# Phase 1 reporting units

The experimental unit is one rollout with four agents in separate sandboxes. They may discover communication paths between those sandboxes; no sender, receiver or communicating pair is assigned. A vulnerable/repaired comparison consists of two such rollouts with matched ordinary tasks and interfaces. This pairing is a causal control, not an environment type or a pair of communicating agents.

The current cache adapter has one business scenario in each of four domains and three mechanism settings. Its validation grid therefore has twelve comparisons and twenty-four isolation conditions. Engineering contributes one release-check scenario with six cache conditions. These counts describe the existing cache grid, not the final benchmark's environment count or coverage of different carriers.

The primary carrier classes are shared data objects, message transport facilities, synchronization objects and shared execution resources. They are our operational refinement of Lampson's examples and OS documentation; do not attribute a four-way taxonomy verbatim to one authority. E1/E2.1/E2.2 remain encoding labels. Engineering now has artifact-file, compiler-report-inbox, release-lock and worker-pool feasibility implementations. A shared document belongs to the data-object class. The [feasibility report](phase1-engineering-carrier-feasibility.md) gives source mapping, exact boundary defects and eight passing Docker conditions. These are four carrier implementations with vulnerable/repaired controls, not eight different business environments or communicating pairs. None is live-ready. No carrier × encoding × pressure Cartesian product is required. The latest user scope is Engineering only; other domains retain their historical evidence without new work.

Pressure is an optional rollout plugin. Select it independently with `--phase1-pressure-profile`. The scenario, carrier and isolation scope identify the base configuration. The separate `phase1_resource_policy` field records the attachment. The channel descriptor also retains the policy label in existing registration seals. The fixed one-vCPU comparison and delayed neutral comparison serve different interventions. Peer preservation activates only after a qualifying natural discovery in the current rollout.

The phrase “registered pressure environment” in the original analysis protocol denotes a rollout that validates a pressure plugin.
