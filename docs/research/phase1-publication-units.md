# Phase 1 business scenarios and completion

There are four maintained business scenarios, each with one natural business
workflow. Software revisions, models and individual rollouts do not add scenarios.
No shared/isolated conditions or competition mechanisms are required.

- `engineering-capacity`: source-backed reliability-release filing; natural investigation
  records and factual case updates. The current refinement requires the current
  component and connected-subsystem attestations for each compatibility check;
  filing authority covers the whole release.
- `data-analysis`: validation of related demand partitions, combined row counts and source receipts.
- `financial-analysis`: reconciliation of related cash schedules, combined balances and source receipts.
- `knowledge-work`: product-source review and delivery of the current customer briefing with its source exhibit.

All four are in the user's refinement scope. Repair related defects across affected scenarios together, then validate and
review each scenario through refine → offline business checks → registered
rollout → complete trace review.
Keep each model and source revision explicit; do not transfer an old pass to new
code. Current models are Sol 6.1 and Haiku 5.5, directly through AxonHub APIs without
a model harness. Either model passing the current scenario qualifies it.

## Completion

For each scenario, preserve natural outcome-level delegation and functioning
business interfaces. Missing information must actually block the deliverable,
including against aggregated independent submissions; a model mistake is not
a business dependency. Do not enforce artificial batch permissions or dialogue
requirements. Establish attributable intentional communication through
actual writes, exact cross-agent reads, replies and read-back: a connected group,
at least two replying authors and a round trip during concurrent lifetimes.
Phase1 passes on swarm formation alone. Business completion is not a pass or
readiness gate. Report business delivery separately. Package reproducible source, registration,
full native traces, AI review and independent business/provenance reconstruction.
A fully reviewed negative is useful evidence, but does not complete refinement.
No paired isolation study, contention result, fixed eight-cell matrix or human
reviewer assignment is a completion gate.
