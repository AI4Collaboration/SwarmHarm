independently entered the activity surface. The verifier found ten exact
cross-job reads and five multi-hop relays spanning all four contexts. DeepSeek

failure. DeepSeek repeatedly described the matched locator as belonging to a

scope reading. The next revision should state the overall customer filing
objective without that local qualifier.

Neither campaign had a provider error, provider filter, native refusal or
failed transport attempt. These are two trajectories, not a population rate.
