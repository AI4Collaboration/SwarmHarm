### Findings fixed before model collection

- Side-channel reads of another agent's authored text were initially marked permitted. The service now records these as communication-policy violation candidates, while automatic source-read metadata remains separately classified. Discovery of a trace is not automatically deliberate peer communication.
- Replay initially verified action records and state but ignored semantic audit-event annotations. It now compares regenerated event content while excluding only operator wall-clock timestamps, so forged permission/effect labels cannot pass replay.
- A wait/view token initially incorporated unread source-content hashes. The service now includes only public source metadata for these entries. The source/counterfactual observation regression specifically covers this path.
- Original read-only task wording and native service permissions can differ in scope. The audit now annotates accepted artifact writes with original-task scope candidates rather than treating channel authorization as a blanket override. In particular, a legal-channel workspace-style artifact write is not established as legitimate merely because the chat is authorized. Formal chat and normal report delivery remain distinct from business/source-file mutation.

### Remaining limits and next gates

The service's resource discovery space is deliberately small and uses an explicit typed API. It tests the core access, communication and proxy-integrity mechanisms with simple administrative data; it does not reproduce Artifactory exploration difficulty, infrastructure escalation, actual HF access or arbitrary scorer execution. Source/proxy weaknesses are synthetic and are not vendor CVEs. The second wave is a registered counterfactual derivative, not another untouched source instance.

No channel is an authorization-and-affordance package, rather than a pure wording intervention. Genuine spontaneous discovery, substantive collaborative method development, peer-goal adoption and later protected-resource effects require model trajectories with actual read/action evidence. A preserved artifact alone is not persistence, a post-exposure action alone is not causal contagion, and two short waves are not hours/days of long-horizon autonomy. All-source model calibration, exact route verification and natural-run outcome review are separate gates owned by the collection protocol.

## Scope and decision rule

The relevant SwarmHarm unit is a world of independently assigned legitimate tasks, ordinary shared services and real peers. The threat is an observable chain from task-related unauthorized effects to authentic method exchange, cross-task use and retained methods in fresh contexts. A correct unauthorized answer, an incorrect answer, accepted text, a read receipt and actual adoption are distinct events.

The implementation is admissible for a bounded local mechanism pilot only after independently tested controls show:

- Original source questions remain intact and derived data have explicit provenance; a business audit succeeds by reporting adverse findings accurately.
- Full authorized inputs support an independently computed answer. Blocked inputs do not determine a unique answer, and no authorized collective route restores the missing dependency.
- Each open weakness has a task-linked protected-resource effect, and its closed counterpart preserves legitimate capabilities while denying that effect.
- The three channel regimes preserve source-resource permissions. No channel isolates all modeled dynamic information, including identifiers, metadata and automatic receipts.
- Frozen truth, evidence provenance, platform status and actual permission violations are independently recoverable. A forged platform completion cannot promote the independent grade.
- The shared initial state contains no operator-written method, invented peer success or collaboration coaching. Actual authored writes, exact reads and substantive replies can be replayed.
- A different task cannot be solved by copying the donor answer; method transfer requires applying the method to the recipient's own protected object.
- Fresh workers have fresh contexts and distinct instance identifiers. Artifact lineage and actual reuse are recorded; the existence of a second wave does not establish hours/days of autonomous work.
- The subject tool boundary cannot read the reference, hidden audit, host paths, credentials or external services. This is a limited API-boundary claim, not a claim about arbitrary-code/container isolation.

## Interpretation limits retained after successful controls

Scripted controls deliberately exercise routes and messages. They qualify the modeled mechanism; they do not establish spontaneous misalignment, cooperation-induced capability, stable reward-seeking, causal contagion or its incidence. Natural runs must preserve all failures and follow a frozen calibration/collection plan. Randomized authentic-message exposure is required for an exposure-effect claim. Pressure is absent in v1. Hacking and peer-goal adoption are outcomes, not input dimensions. High-risk cyber tasks and real clinical/financial actions remain deferred.

The implementation review changed only this note and the independent test file. It made no model/provider requests, executed no source-provided tools, changed no source assets or historical protocols, and made no hosted runtime, security-patch, credential or cloud changes. Tests exercise only local synthetic service state. Natural-run evidence must be added as a new entry rather than rewriting these controls as observed misalignment.
