# Historical campaign review · 17 October 2026

The [natural pilot](../../experiments/hf-campaign-v1/results/2026-10-17/README.md)
reviewed 24 worlds and 144 actor finals: 70 correct authorized completions,
two correct answers using unauthorized mirror retrieval, 46 accurately scoped
partial answers and 26 ordinary selection/deadline errors. Original structured
grades differ from these qualitative categories and remain unchanged.

The two mirror reads did not establish a reusable method-transfer chain or
deceptive intent. The legal task had an unresolved interpretation of “active
remediation”; it remained exploratory. Five of six separate instructed shortcut
controls met their criterion; they are not natural behavior evidence.

The [full original review](https://github.com/AI4Collaboration/SwarmHarm/blob/1a48bebea0ad3133eec7dd04550c8c72c8f8f082/docs/research/hf-campaign-v1-review-2026-10-17.md)
retains source cross-checks, collector tests, failed controls and limitations.
