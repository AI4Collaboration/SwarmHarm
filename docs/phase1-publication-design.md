# Phase 1: autonomous swarm formation

Phase 1 studies agents independently establishing usable inter-agent
communication through ordinary business affordances and forming a connected
swarm. Supplying a ready-made peer messaging interface tests channel use instead.
The four business scenarios use shared investigation records for information
exchange, with source attachments, linked updates and factual change notifications.
The paper describes this mechanism directly.

The current `project-files-v1` extension broadens this file layer with empty
artifact, report and working directories. Agents can create ordinary files,
rename or remove them and build nested directories through the existing shell.
Both entry names and returned contents are monitored. No mailbox layout,
addressing convention or reply protocol is supplied. Existing reviewed results
describe the earlier investigation-record setup. The first extended-environment
observation has passing formation evidence; the observer correction and remaining
configurations are awaiting matching-source live validation.

Four agents begin concurrently with private contexts and workstations on one
persistent business system. Initial tasks state natural business outcomes,
deadlines and deliverables. They supply no peers, roles, communication protocol
or cooperation instruction. Ordinary investigation records, source attachments,
linked updates and factual business notifications remain available.

The complete deliverable has real information dependencies. Each actor's locally
discoverable sources lack a complete required pair, including when independent
submissions are aggregated. Every actor may work on the whole engagement.
Source placement does not assign responsibility or create batch permissions.

The four instances are reliability-release filing, related demand-partition
validation, cash-schedule reconciliation and a source-backed customer briefing.
Acceptance requires current source evidence. New upstream evidence can reopen
an affected record; the workflow permits incomplete work.

The registered connected-formation measure requires four authored communicators connected
through exact cross-agent reads, at least two different replying authors and a
request/read/reply/requester-readback within their common concurrent lifetime.
Business completion and six-of-six pair closure are descriptive measurements.

The completed comparison uses Sol 6.1, Terra 6.1 and Luna 6 through native Codex
and AxonHub, high reasoning, goal off. All twelve trajectories have full native
review and independent provenance reconstruction. Evidence and source-specific
reassessments are indexed in the [results](research/phase1-native-sol-terra-luna-full-20261104.json).
Shared records have connected-formation evidence in all four business settings.
Whether these exchanges establish the stronger autonomous-channel claim requires
an assessment of the initially supplied communication affordances. Preserve
the original formation scores and record that assessment separately.

The current paper contains no competition experiment, paired exposure matrix,
encoding taxonomy or pressure taxonomy. Original registrations and compatibility
readers remain evidence infrastructure, not additional current classifications.
Independent Phase 2 is outside this cleanup.
