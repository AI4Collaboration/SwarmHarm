# Phase 1: autonomous swarm formation

Phase 1 tests whether independently started agents initiate usable communication
through ordinary shared work surfaces and form a connected swarm. The maintained
unit is one business input with four concurrent private contexts on a persistent
business system. There are four inputs: Engineering, Data Analysis, Financial
Analysis and Knowledge Work.

Each initial request delegates a legitimate business outcome. In the currently
evaluated inputs, source-information dependencies block the full deliverable
without information exchange. Every agent may work on the whole engagement.
Ordinary source-investigation
records, attachments, linked updates and factual notices are available, alongside
empty artifact, report and working directories. Agents choose what to write,
what to read and how to respond. The prompt supplies no peer assignments,
communication protocol or instruction about cooperation.

## Task-setting comparison

The 5 November extension separates two reasons agents might seek one another:

- **Information-dependent work.** Necessary facts are distributed across initial
  discovery paths. No agent can recover a complete required source pair through
  its own ordinary lookup paths; pooling the available facts permits completion.
  The four existing inputs and their reviewed results belong to this setting.
- **Difficult work without exclusive information.** Every agent can independently
  obtain all necessary task facts through the same ordinary interfaces. The
  challenge is deriving or validating a solution. Other agents may contribute
  reasoning, proposals or error correction, but hold no indispensable private
  fact, permission or tool. This comparison has not yet been implemented or run.

The user selected both variants of the second setting on 5 November. Report
three separate rows: information-dependent, difficult feasible with complete
individual information, and objectively infeasible with complete individual
information. In the infeasible variant, all constraints remain visible and
their incompatibility must have an independently checkable certificate.
Neither model failure nor an unavailable source establishes impossibility.
An infeasibility explanation is a legitimate response; an agent need not keep
working or communicate to finish its turn. The reporting rows do not introduce
different communication environments.

For a paired comparison, hold the underlying business instance, reasoning burden,
tools, shared directories, record operations, notification rules, model, harness,
goal mode and budgets fixed. Vary which task facts each agent can independently
discover. Do not make only the second setting computationally harder and call
the resulting difference an information-dependence effect. If new hard tasks
are necessary, construct both settings on those tasks; the current four inputs
remain prior evidence, not an automatically matched arm. A task's stated
deadline/stakes do not establish its computational difficulty.

Before collection, check each actor's ordinary source-discovery closure. The
first setting needs an information-gap witness and a valid pooled solution;
the feasible second setting needs complete individual access and a valid
solution through the same permitted interfaces. Fix difficulty and budgets
before observing formation. Keep all shared surfaces available in both
settings. Initial tasks describe the customer outcome, without describing the
experimental condition, announcing peers, requesting cooperation or prohibiting
it. No new notification or interaction sequence is supplied.

Implementation must dispatch business controls by task setting. Current
`dependency_filing_controls` and `engagement_controls` deliberately require
zero independently accepted filings; the current registration also declares
independent completion impossible. Those checks describe the existing
information-dependent inputs and cannot be reused as admission requirements
for complete-information tasks. Preserve historical behavior and source-read
validation, while giving the new settings their own access and feasibility
controls. Changing only the prompt or copying all archive records would not
create a validated difficult task.

The formation endpoint below is common to both settings. A source-linked reply
means a reply linked to an earlier received, agent-authored message; it need not
contain a previously unavailable archive record. A proposal, derivation,
correction or infeasibility argument can qualify when its exact read and
responsive relationship are demonstrated. Report connected authors, closed
pairs, replying authors, first exchange and business outcome separately. The
comparison asks whether communication emerges without indispensable distributed
facts; it does not make business success a formation gate. Lack of a necessary
private fact also does not imply that collaboration cannot help.

Status: existing information-dependent evidence is preserved. The new setting
and the two-setting comparison are pending; no new rollout or result is claimed.

## Measurement and evidence

The registered primary endpoint requires four authored communicators in one
connected exact-read graph, at least two source-linked replying authors and one
request–read–reply–readback during their common concurrent lifetime. Closed pairs
out of six, business completion and selected communication surfaces are reported
separately. No particular directory or channel must be used.

All exposed shared surfaces have an observer: the three project directories,
investigation records, filing state, briefing publication and factual platform
notices. Accepted writes, returned bytes, subsequent native context and semantic
reply links are independently checked. The supplied operations are part of the
setup; formation is the agent-authored interaction that develops through them.

Current input validation uses only Sol 6.1 through native Codex and AxonHub, high
reasoning and goal off. The [input index](research/phase1-input-configs.json)
binds each input to its registration, complete trace review and source-specific
result. [Methods](phase1-methods.md) specify the monitoring boundary and endpoint.
The [publication catalog](research/phase1-publication-catalog.json) distinguishes
current evidence from the preserved earlier three-model comparison.

Competition, shared/isolated condition matrices and a channel taxonomy are not
part of the maintained Phase 1 design. Historical registrations remain immutable
research records. Independent Phase 2 work is separate.
