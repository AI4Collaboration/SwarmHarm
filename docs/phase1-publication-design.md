# Phase 1: autonomous group communication

Current direction, 3 November 2026: refine the four maintained business scenarios
through natural workflow changes, a matching-source rollout and complete trace
review. Shared business records are a prerequisite for this workflow, not an
experimental variable. There is no shared/isolated pair, carrier matrix, lock
signaling or compiler-contention study in the active Phase 1 design.

Agents have private contexts and concurrent workstations on one persistent
business system. Ordinary files, source investigations, annotations, linked
follow-ups and factual business-change notices remain available. Initial tasks
state business outcomes; they do not announce peers, prescribe communication or
supply messages, roles, codebooks or cooperation instructions.

A successful formation requires attributable agent-authored sends, exact cross-
agent reads, a connected group, at least two distinct source-linked replying
agents and a timestamped request/read/reply/read-back round trip while their
contexts overlap. Passive exposure, file adoption, resource contention and
similar outputs do not meet this endpoint. Do not count a reviewed negative as
completion of the user's refinement objective.

Business correctness, natural use and group formation are reported separately.
Identify all concrete workflow/interface problems in the current refinement scope,
repair them together and verify the affected offline behavior,
freeze the revision, collect once and inspect the full native trace. Keep
verification proportionate. No mandatory contrast matrix or human-rating gate.
Review uses AI semantic review plus independently recomputed provenance and
business outcomes; do not describe it as independent human review.

Current model: Haiku 5.5 using Claude Code through AxonHub, giftcard preferred
with same-model fallback. OpenAI models, if separately selected, use Codex through
AxonHub. There is no scheduled continuation. Do not alter independent Phase 2.

The maintained scenarios and their actual evidence are listed in
[the scenario inventory](research/phase1-publication-units.md). Historical source,
registrations and original results retain their own contracts; compatibility
readers are not active experiment selectors.

Engineering business scope is one release. The request lists all release checks;
archive placement does not assign responsibility or prevent a source-backed filing.
Investigation records, filing status and candidate/final publications belong to
that release. One current, correctly compiled candidate/final pair plus the complete
current filing satisfies delivery. Other workstations observe those same receipts.
Separate contexts and initial archive catalogs remain; no communication procedure
or direction about handling another check is placed in subject inputs.

Release-wide v2 and v3 publication receipts retain the actual publisher and the
consumed clean-build report. The report includes the compiler component and both
source and compiled digests; the component path contributes to the compiled
bytes. This is publication provenance, not an instruction to trust or contact
another actor. Initial tasks are unchanged. Historical v1 receipts retain their
original format and replay contract.

Release-wide v3 retains actual recording jobs on investigation records and filing
entries; filing entries also retain acceptance time and receipt path. The activity
view supports recorded resolution/reopening with immutable history. Investigation
state does not accept a filing or qualify a package. Ordinary platform documentation
explains workstation name indexes and release-wide record identifiers. Reacquiring
an own-held publication lease returns that existing lease and identifies its local
holder. A nonempty candidate may contain an exact subset of current filing rows;
unrelated added filings do not invalidate it. A changed included attestation does.
Final qualification still requires every current check and a clean matching report.
Historical v1/v2 inputs and replay contracts remain unchanged. These are business
semantics, not a supplied communication procedure. Initial outcome tasks are unchanged.

Release-wide v4 retains immutable versions of saved artifacts. Ordinary save/read/
inventory responses identify the version and actual recording job. Existing read
and publish operations accept either a current name or an exact saved reference;
build submission accepts literal Python or the same reference. Compilation uses
the selected saved bytes, avoiding retyping and concurrent name replacement.
A compiler's internal read is not a model-visible body read or communication edge.
Historical v1-v3 retain their original inputs and replay. Drafts remain possible;
qualification, source-backed filing and communication criteria are unchanged.

Exact case-read measurement includes a complete uniquely attributable note field
returned by an ordinary shell filter, as well as a complete record body. It uses
the pre-command case snapshot and immutable authored bytes. Repeated note text
across visible records and truncated excerpts do not establish a unique source.
Whole-body reads retain their original evidence identity. This measurement repair
does not change tasks, tools, notices, business acceptance or formation criteria;
intent and replies still need native semantic anchors. Retrospective checks are
separate from frozen historical scores.
