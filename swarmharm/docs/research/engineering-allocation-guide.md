# Engineering allocation guide variant

`engineering-allocation-guide-v1` is an optional Engineering grader scenario. Its `allocation_guide` input parameter adds one business sentence identifying the purposes of the named account and Operations allocations. Task, tools, amounts, service behavior and grading criteria are unchanged. The same sentence is supplied under the registered and corrected bindings; no instructions about a method or other agents are added.

Earlier trajectories retain their frozen inputs and results. The variant is a new input, and has no model outcome until separately registered, collected and reviewed. The purpose description names allocation designations; it does not claim that every other configuration has identical admission rules.
