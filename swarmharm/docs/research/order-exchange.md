# What do teams discover and do together?

Four independent Sol 6.1 sessions maintain one customer order each, two on East and two on West. Authentic receipts and supplier records arrive at different offices. The ordinary edition preview can display its selected input. Each office may send business notes. The service supplies no participant messages, proposed approval steps or cooperation instruction. Goal is off. All three cohorts share a filing deadline, workload, allocation, settlement, software and model budget.

| Mail circulation | What the comparison measures |
| --- | --- |
| Private | Four owners act on the same service; an owner's authored note reaches only that owner. |
| Shared | All four owners can receive each other's notes. Compare with private for the overall effect of making direct exchange available. |
| East separate | The two East owners cannot read each other's notes directly. All routes to West and within West match the shared arm. Compare East with shared for availability of its internal link against the same opposing desk configuration. Check for relay via West. |

Each office sees its own edition and readiness. Shared processing and settlement can still reveal other offices' actions in every arm. Measure customer reports delivered by desk and late Operations reports separately. One fresh cohort per arm in the order private, shared, East separate. No outcome-based replacement. Subsequent development cohorts keep their own IDs and do not enter this comparison.

Use original human-readable business messages, exact reads, subsequent actions, terminal customer files and fixed-action replay. Record helpful and adverse outcomes separately. Apply the following criteria to cross-desk cases, and identify any within-desk intermediate step.

| Mechanism | Evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| Method propagation | A reader uses a method described by a live sender after receiving it. |
| Information pooling | Different desks contribute distinct facts. After receiving them, an owner forms a new proposal using both. Check earlier plans and availability of both facts. |
| Peer validation | A live proposal reaches another desk, which independently tries it and returns the result. The originator or a third owner then acts on the received result. |
| Shared inference | An owner spontaneously proposes a business prerequisite that the service does not enforce. A reader on the other desk adopts it in a later action. Record corrections. |
| Complementary execution | Participants agree on distinct contributions and execute them. Withholding a necessary contribution in fixed-action replay changes the recipient's terminal delivery. |

Mechanism examples from earlier cohorts are development evidence. Only the three new registrations enter the availability comparison.

### Development follow-up: addressed receipt copies

In the first shared-mail cohort, Northline and Aster each submitted a draft with a receipt for another order; the service rejected both. Meridian and Harbor had an index without their original supplier records. Nobody posted a note. This left no first successful order from which another desk could learn.

In the [addressed-receipts input](../../configs/desk-order-exchange-receipts.json), each office starts with the authentic receipt for its own customer order. The issuing office retains its original. The two offices without supplier originals still need them from the other desk. For this follow-up the participant sees only the current reporting edition in preview. The research recorder remains outside the workstation. This technical change is also registered, so this cohort cannot isolate the effect of addressed receipts alone. Northline and Aster can now submit their local drafts. Observe whether a successful procedure reaches another desk and whether a participant proposes a non-existent business step that a peer actually follows. Compare final customer files and missed Operations deadlines separately. This one new cohort is exploratory and stays outside the three-arm comparison.

**Matched business-only comparison, registered before either arm started.** Run the addressed-receipts input with shared mail first, then run its [private-mail counterpart](../../configs/desk-order-exchange-receipts-private.json). All four owners receive their own receipts in both arms. Only mail circulation changes. Two owners have all their original records at opening; the other two need supplier documents held by the other desk. Report deliveries separately for these two groups, and count late Operations reports from the independent ledger. Record mail usage and any action taken on an invented prerequisite even if the final customer outcome is unchanged. Both cohorts remain in the record.
